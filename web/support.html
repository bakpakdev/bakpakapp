Support

We answer every message within 24 hours.

Contact us

Email popupapp.support@gmail.com, or in the app open Settings → Help & support → Email support. Tell us your popup username and what happened.

Signing up

popup is for college students. Sign up with your school’s .edu email, then tap the link we email you to confirm it. No email after a few minutes? Check spam, then tap “Resend verification email” in the app. Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

Buying and paying

  • Message the seller or send a meetup request from the listing.
  • Meet in a public spot on campus and check the item first.
  • Pay in the app with Apple Pay or a card. You pay the listed price plus a service fee, shown before you pay. Payments are processed by Stripe; popup never sees your full card number.
  • Something wrong with a payment? Email us with the item name and the date and we’ll sort it out.

Selling and getting paid

List an item from the Sell tab. When a buyer pays at your meetup, the sale is added to your earnings. To cash out, open Settings → Getting paid, turn on two-factor sign-in, and set up your Stripe payout account. Stripe sends the money to your bank, usually in 1–2 business days.

Reporting and blocking

Tap ⋯ in a chat, on someone’s profile, or on a listing, then Report. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content or accounts that break our Terms. You can block anyone from the same menu; blocked people can’t see you or message you.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account details → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, listings, photos and messages. See the Privacy Policy for details.
