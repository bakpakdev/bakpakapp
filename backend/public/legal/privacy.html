Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

popup is a campus marketplace where college students buy and sell clothing and meet up on campus. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: popupapp.support@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your college email, name, username, school, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), and optional profile photo, bio and style preferences.
  • Listings and photos: the photos, titles, descriptions, prices and other details you post.
  • Messages and meetups: chats with other students, offers, meetup requests, the campus spot and time you pick, and reports or blocks you make.
  • Purchases and payouts: what you bought or sold, amounts and fees. Card and bank details are entered with Stripe and stay with Stripe; popup never sees your full card number.
  • Reviews, follows and likes you leave on the app.
  • Device and usage basics: settings such as dark mode and notification choices, and technical logs our servers keep to run and secure the service.

We don’t use your data for advertising, we don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we don’t sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep popup limited to students at your campus.
  • To show your listings and profile to other students, and deliver your messages and meetup updates.
  • To process payments and pay sellers.
  • To identify a clothing item from a photo when you use the Sell tab scanner.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, stop fraud and enforce our Terms.
  • To send account emails such as email confirmation and password reset.

Who we share it with

  • Other students: your username, school, profile photo, bio, listings, reviews and (if you allow them in Settings → Privacy) your sold items and follower counts.
  • Service providers that run parts of popup for us: Supabase (accounts, database, photo storage), Stripe (payments and seller payouts), OpenAI (the clothing scanner receives the photo you choose to scan), Resend (account emails) and Render (hosting our server). They may only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons: when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time, and choose what others see in Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Settings → Preferences or in iOS Settings.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile or a chat.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account details. This removes your profile, listings and messages; we keep payment records only as long as the law and our payment provider require.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we delete or anonymize your personal data, except records we must keep for payments, taxes, fraud prevention or legal claims.

Security

Data moves over encrypted connections, and you can turn on two-factor authentication in Settings → Security. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us right away if you think your account was accessed without you.

Age

popup is for college students 18 or older. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
